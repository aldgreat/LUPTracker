♻️ Refund Process

🔒 Attorney-Client Privilege

The Recall Refund Playbook supports our Customer Service team by outlining the refund workflow from intake to validation and completion[cite: 1].

Customer Notification Channels

  • FDA press release (1/14) – Expiration dates 08/2026 to 01/2028 affected[cite: 1].
  • CDC press release / social media (1/14)[cite: 1].
  • Live It Up customer email outreach (Starting 1/15)[cite: 1].
  • Live It Up website footer & Super Greens PDP blog post (Starting 1/26)[cite: 1].

Customer Inquiry Methods

Customers submit claims via Recall Refund Request Form A (requires Order # & Lot # Photo) or Form B (missing Order # / Lot #)[cite: 1]. Manual ticket submissions must be added directly to the refund tracking document[cite: 1].

✔️ Refund Criteria

Valid Refund Requirements

  • Lot Number starting with “A” or “3”[cite: 1].
  • Valid Email Address & Order Number[cite: 1].
  • Valid Picture: Package photo showing Lot Code/Exp Date OR canister/greens in storage[cite: 1].

Refund Policy Rules

  • Applies to active/non-current subscribers and single-time purchasers[cite: 1].
  • Refund covers ONE bag of Super Greens (or unfinished bags from bulk packs)[cite: 1].
  • Full refund including original shipping and tax[cite: 1].

👍 Refund Request Validation

  1. Log into the Vibe Refunding Portal, set Assignee to your name, filter Status to "New", and sort by Email[cite: 1].
  2. Verify Lot #: Must start with "A" or "3"[cite: 1].
  3. Look up Order # in Shopify to check unit counts[cite: 1].
  4. Cross-check photo proof in Asana[cite: 1].
  5. Update Status to Confirmed, Denied, or Follow-up (never leave as "New")[cite: 1].

🛍️ Replacement Bags

Applies to requests with Status: Confirmed and Type: Replacement Bag[cite: 1].

  • Unique, single-use code sent via personalized redemption link[cite: 1].
  • All allotted bags must be claimed in a single order[cite: 1].
  • Super Greens items are pre-orders shipping Summer 2026; other products ship immediately[cite: 1].

➕ Store Credit

Active Subscribers (Recharge)

Store credit is applied to Recharge accounts[cite: 1]. Subscribers toggle auto-apply in their account portal[cite: 1].

Non-Subscribers (Shopify)

Store credit is issued to Shopify[cite: 1]. Customers log in via the purple Shop icon at checkout to apply store credit[cite: 1].

💰 Cash Refunds

Cash refunds are remitted on a rolling schedule[cite: 1]. Customers receive confirmation emails once approved[cite: 1].

🅰️ Amazon Orders Protocol

  • Official Amazon Store: Offer a replacement bag first[cite: 1]. If declined, post details in #amazon-orders-recall Slack channel for processing[cite: 1].
  • 3rd Party Resellers: Direct customer to retailer[cite: 1]. If escalated, require invoice + photo proof for manual PayPal/check refund[cite: 1].
